SONAR FLY · PLAYER PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This policy describes how Sonar Fly (the “Game”) handles information when you play.

1. Information collected

No player account or personal contact information is required to play. The Game may store high scores, progress, and settings such as audio and haptic preferences locally on your device.

When advertising is enabled, Google AdMob and its partners may process IP addresses, device information, advertising identifiers (where permitted), ad views and interactions, and diagnostics. Processing varies by SDK configuration, region, device settings, and consent choices.

2. How information is used

Information may be used to provide gameplay features, remember settings, deliver and measure advertisements, enable optional ad rewards, maintain security, prevent fraud, and diagnose technical issues.

3. Google AdMob and rewarded advertising

Sonar Fly uses Google AdMob, including optional rewarded video ads that may provide an in-game benefit such as reviving a character. Google and advertising partners may process data according to their own policies. See Google's Privacy Policy and Google AdMob policies.

Where legally required, the Game must provide suitable consent choices before applicable advertising data processing. Advertising preferences may also be available in your device settings.

4. Children's privacy

We do not knowingly request names, email addresses, or other personal information directly from children. If the Game is made available to children, applicable protections for child-directed games and advertising, including Google Play Families requirements where relevant, must be followed. Parents or guardians may contact us with privacy concerns.

5. Sharing and third parties

We do not directly sell information provided by players. Third-party advertising providers may independently process information for ad delivery, measurement, security, and fraud prevention, subject to their policies and applicable law.

6. Storage and retention

Locally saved gameplay information typically remains until you clear app data or uninstall the Game. Third-party providers retain data according to their own retention policies.

7. Your choices

You can adjust supported privacy or advertising options in your device and in the Game where provided, clear local app data, uninstall the Game, or contact us about a privacy request.

8. Security

We aim to use reasonable safeguards, although no internet service or electronic storage method is guaranteed completely secure.

9. Policy changes

We may update this page when the Game, third-party integrations, or legal requirements change. The updated date will appear above.
